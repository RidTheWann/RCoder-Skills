## Evaluator methodology and biases

Five layers are defined in `evals/EVALUATION-INDEPENDENCE.md`: deterministic objective checks, test/artifact verification, rubric transcript review, independent model judgment, and human adjudication. Layers A/B are implemented and self-tested. Layer C now blinds arm labels, preserves originals, retains full responses, and supports `--judge-model`. Layers D/E are NOT VERIFIED (no separate authorized model or adjudicator). Same-model judge bias in the retained control is disclosed and has not been retroactively removed.
