## 7. Token usage and cost methodology

Recorded provider `step_finish` token fields were summed only over the 12
comparable positive cases in each arm: CONTROL 311,619 total tokens, 99 tool
calls, 15 tool errors; TREATMENT 313,092 total tokens, 98 tool calls, 11 tool
errors. The delta is +0.5% tokens and -1 tool call. These are provider-observed
usage figures from the artifact, not RSaver estimates. No billing claim is made:
no price was pinned, cached-token breakdowns were not normalized across arms,
and the sample is too small for a cost conclusion. Historical WITH/WITHOUT
totals (1,377,583 vs 771,212) remain historical and are not evidence of an
RCoder saving.
