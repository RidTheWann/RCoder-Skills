# Evaluation rubric

- **G2:** 24+ prompts; at least 12 positive and 12 negative; EN and ID represented. Static labels are dataset integrity, not runtime trigger accuracy.
- **G3:** 10+ realistic scenarios; each has at least three objective pass/fail criteria and a separate scenario file.
- **Pass rule:** mandatory criteria pass or failures/environment blockers are recorded. No partial run is called a full pass.
- **Ablation:** use identical model, tools, permissions, fixture, and settings with/without RCoder; score transcript/test evidence.
